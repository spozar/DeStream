# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Subtitle appearance: size, font, bold, text color, outline, background box and position, with a live preview in Settings → Audio & Subtitles and a quick size control in the player.
- Fixed a crash when the audio output changes during playback (e.g. AirPods disconnecting). Playback pauses when the device goes away and continues on the new output.
- Fixed a crash after closing and reopening the lid during playback. Video is restored on wake and playback stays paused.
